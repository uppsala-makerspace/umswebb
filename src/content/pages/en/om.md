Uppsala Makerspace is a politically and religiously independent non-profit
association based in Uppsala. It was founded on 15 September 2014.

We want to give the people of Uppsala — young and old — who are interested in
making things a place to meet, to be inspired and to help each other along in
the creative process. We welcome making in every kind of material, with or
without machines and tools, and for every purpose: usefulness, innovation,
learning or art.

**Note** that the association only provides the space and the machines, and that
all help comes from the goodwill of other members. We cannot guarantee that
everyone gets taught how to use our tools. It is up to each of us to acquire the
skills needed for the tools we want to use. That said, we very much want those
with particular skills to share them — do get in touch if you would like to run
a course!
