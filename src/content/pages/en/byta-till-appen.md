You are already a member — this is the guide to getting started with our new
app. **No new payment is needed right now.** You create an account that is
linked to your existing membership, accept the latest liability undertaking,
and carry on using the app as usual.

## You don't have to switch straight away

It is fine to wait until it is time to renew your membership — then you do the
switch and the renewal in one go. But the sooner you switch, the sooner you can
unlock the doors yourself and get messages from the board in the app instead of
by email.

## How to do it

The whole flow, with screenshots, is in the guide
[**Existing members — getting started**](https://tutorial.uppsalamakerspace.se/en/app/existingMembers/).
In short:

1. **Install the app** from
   [app.uppsalamakerspace.se](https://app.uppsalamakerspace.se/) — see the
   [installation guide](https://tutorial.uppsalamakerspace.se/en/app/installApp/)
   if you need help.
2. **Check your membership email** with the button in the yellow box on the
   login page. Use the address you gave when you became a member.
3. **Create an account** with that same address — that is how the app connects
   your new account to your existing membership. (Or pick _Continue with
   Google_ if your membership address is a Google account.)
4. **Confirm the email** through the link we send.
5. **Accept the liability undertaking** in the app.
6. **Done!** Open the side menu to find _My account_ and _My profile_. If you
   have lab access you can now unlock the doors straight from the app when you
   are on site.

## When it is time to renew

Renewal now happens inside the app — Swish, the same flow as for a new
membership. There is a step-by-step guide in
[**Renew your membership**](https://tutorial.uppsalamakerspace.se/en/app/renewMembership/).

No Swish? Get in touch with
[kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se](mailto:kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se) and we
will sort the renewal out by bank giro instead. You still need the app — the
manual payment only replaces the Swish step itself.

## Help with the app

Questions about installing it, or about how the app works? Join **`#app`** in
[our Slack](https://uppsalamakerspace.slack.com/) — both the board and other
members who have been through the same flow are there.

For anything else, email the board at
[kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se](mailto:kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se).

## My email is not in the register

If you enter your address in the check box and no email arrives, we probably
have a different address for you. Try another one, or email
[kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se](mailto:kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se) and we
will help you.
