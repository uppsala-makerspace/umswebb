Our space has the tools and the room to build, construct and create, both small
things and large. It is also where you will often meet other creative and
knowledgeable people.

## Map of our space

The space has two floors. Click the floor behind to switch, and a room to see
what is in it.

## Opening hours

We have an open evening once a week, when new lab members can also get an
introduction to how entry and the routines work — see our
[calendar](/en/calendar/) for the dates. If you want to come and go as you
please, become a lab member; our [membership page](/en/become-a-member/)
explains what that involves.

There are always one or more **hosts** on site on the open evenings — members
who keep the space open, show you around and answer questions about becoming a
member and what it means. They also introduce new lab members to the space and
our routines.

## Getting here

Our makerspace is in Ekebybruk, west of central Uppsala. The space is at ground
level, and there is both paid and free parking nearby (see further down the
page). We offer workspaces and storage for our lab members, and there are tools
and machines for working on whatever you are making.

## Parking

There is both paid and free parking near the space. Here are the options.

### Ekebybruk 6M (20 m — paid)

Right next to the space there is a paid car park: 10 kr per hour and 40 kr per
day. You can pay with the MobilPark app, or by card in the machine.

![](/uploads/wp/ekeby_parkering.jpg)

### By the Coop supermarket (300 m — 2 hours free)

Next to the Coop there is a car park where you can stay free of charge for two
hours. As far as we have seen and experienced, no parking disc is required.
