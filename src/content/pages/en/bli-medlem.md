Uppsala Makerspace is open to everyone — beginners and seasoned makers
alike. As a member you are welcome at our open evenings; as a **lab
member** you also get your own access to the space whenever you like,
plus storage, consumables and our tool pool.

The whole sign-up — account, payment and liability undertaking — now
happens in our app.

## Getting started in the app

Open **[app.uppsalamakerspace.se](https://app.uppsalamakerspace.se/)** on
your phone. The app is a web app that you install straight from the page
(no App Store or Google Play needed). As soon as you are in, the app asks
you to pick a membership, pay and accept the liability undertaking.

A detailed walkthrough with screenshots is in the guide
[**New members — getting started**](https://tutorial.uppsalamakerspace.se/en/app/newMembers/).
Need help installing it? See
[**Install the app**](https://tutorial.uppsalamakerspace.se/en/app/installApp/).

Stuck? Ask in the **`#app`** channel in
[our Slack](https://uppsalamakerspace.slack.com/). Both the board and other
members who have been through the same flow hang out there.

## How it works

From opening the app to unlocking the door yourself:

1. **Create an account** with your email (or continue with Google).
2. **Confirm your email** through the link we send you.
3. **Tell us about yourself** — your name at the least. Age, gender and
   RFID tag are optional but help the association.
4. **Pick a membership** — tick family or reduced price if either applies
   to you.
5. **Pay with Swish** straight in the app — on the same phone as Swish, or
   by QR code to another device.
6. **Accept the liability undertaking** in the app.
7. **Wait for the board's approval** — we approve as quickly as we can,
   usually within a few days.
8. **Done!** You can now unlock the doors with the app.

## If you cannot use Swish

No Swish, or need to pay some other way? Get in touch with the board at
[kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se](mailto:kansliet@uppsalamakerspace.se) and
we will help you manually. You can also pay by **bank giro 584-8056** —
put your name and email in the message and we will register the membership
for you.

Note that you **still need to use the app** even if you pay manually. That
is where you accept the liability undertaking, unlock the doors and get
messages from the board — the payment step is the only part we move outside
the app in this case.

## If you are under 18

- **Under 15** you need an adult with you in the space (*).
- **Under 18** you may not use rotating tools without an adult, and you
  cannot let in others who are not lab members themselves.

\* Responsible young people from about 12 can get a lab membership after
consulting a guardian, with the guardian's signature on the liability
undertaking and a special introduction with the guardian present.
